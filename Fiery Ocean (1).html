Fiery Ocean

What if posting was no longer instant? Kind comments go straight through. A comment that reads as unkind has to pass an emotional checkpoint first: a pause, a look at the consequences, a puzzle, a chance to rewrite, and a final warning.

Follow the flow

  1. 1Landing screen with the feed
  2. 2Press and hold Like, then drag left or right across the options
  3. 3Tap the comment icon to open comments
  4. 4Drag the comments down to the normal view
  5. 5Open Live and pick a filter
  6. 6Open comments, type your crazy, what you said is nonsense and send it

How Live works

The Live menu lists only what the current post has received. The first post has unkind comments. The flower post has none, but gets critique. New comments keep arriving, so counts tick up and new options can appear. Type your own comment to see where it lands.

The checkpoint

  1. 1Press and hold the orb to cool it down
  2. 2Hold on. Stop and reflect
  3. 3Say it to her face, and slide through time
  4. 4Solve the case
  5. 5Still want to send it? Rewrite it kindly, or not
  6. 6Defuse the sentence
  7. 7Choose a door: softer or as is
  8. 8“Gosh, I can’t fool you” and hold to send

Send three unkind comments and commenting pauses for 30 seconds.
